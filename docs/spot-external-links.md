# Spot webcams and weather links

Curated from locally supplied links, with duplicates removed. These are provider pages, not proposed embed URLs. The linked pages were checked on 25 September 2026 for page identity; live video playback and long-term availability were not verified. “Nearby” means useful context, not a camera or sensor at the app spot.
